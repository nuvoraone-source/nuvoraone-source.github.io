TrennCalc

The calculation app for partition systems. Work out weights and lifting weight right on your phone instead of by hand or in spreadsheets.

  • Calculates technology weight, curtain weight, total weight and lifting weight
  • Available as a Lite and a Pro version
  • Results can be exported as a PNG image
  • The language follows your phone settings

MouseMeter

Shows how much you really do at the PC: how far the mouse travels, how often you click and how much you type.

  • Measures mouse distance, speed and your personal record
  • Counts clicks (left, right, middle), keystrokes and scroll wheel movements
  • For the keyboard only the number of keystrokes is counted, never the text you type
  • Statistics by day, week, month and year
  • Portable: just start the file, no installation needed
  • Your data stays local on your PC, nothing is uploaded
Download for Windows On GitHub

Claveo

A password manager with a data vault. The name comes from the Latin “clavis”, the key.

  • Manages your passwords in one place
  • The vault also stores other everyday data, for example bank accounts, family data and documents
  • Data is meant to be stored strongly encrypted throughout
  • Android first, an iOS version is planned for later
  • The app is currently being published on Google Play
